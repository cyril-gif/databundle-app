Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take? +

Most orders are delivered within 10 minutes. In rare cases of network congestion, it can take up to 24 hours.

What happens if I enter the wrong number? +

We are unable to issue refunds for data sent to an incorrect number, so please double-check before confirming your order.

What if my payment fails? +

If Mobile Money debits you but your order does not update, contact our WhatsApp support with your order reference and we will resolve it quickly.

Do I need to create an account to buy data? +

No. You can buy data as a guest. Creating an account only lets you track order history and earn referral rewards.
