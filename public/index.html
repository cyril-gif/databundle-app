Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How long does delivery take? +

Most orders are delivered within 2-5 minutes after payment confirmation. In rare cases of network congestion, it can take up to 10 minutes. If you don't receive your data within 15 minutes, contact our support.

What payment methods are accepted? +

We accept all major payment methods through Paystack including: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money, all major debit/credit cards, and bank transfers. All payments are securely processed.

What happens if I enter the wrong number? +

We are unable to issue refunds for data sent to an incorrect number, so please double-check before confirming your order. Contact support immediately if you realize the mistake.

What if my payment fails? +

If you are charged but your order does not update, contact our WhatsApp support with your order reference and we will resolve it quickly. Failed payments are automatically refunded within 24 hours.

Do I need to create an account to buy data? +

No. You can buy data as a guest. Creating an account only lets you track order history and earn referral rewards. Sign up is free and takes less than a minute.

How does the BECE result checker work? +

Purchase a checker voucher for GH₵ 5 via Paystack. You'll receive a serial number and PIN via SMS. Enter these along with the candidate's index number to view the result. This is a paid facilitation service, not affiliated with WAEC.
